🧺
Our Story

Born from a pile of dirty laundry

It was a Sunday evening. The laundry basket was overflowing, the washing machine was making that weird noise again, and the iron was nowhere to be found. That's when the idea hit — what if there was a service that just handled all of this? Not a laundromat you had to drive to. Not a vague "we'll pick it up sometime" promise. Something reliable, fast, and genuinely good at taking care of your clothes.

WashReady was born from that frustration. We built a laundry service that works the way it should — you schedule, we pick up, we clean, we deliver. Simple. No downloading apps, no complicated interfaces, no wondering where your order is.

Today, we serve over 10,000 happy customers, process thousands of garments every week, and maintain a 4.9-star rating. And we're just getting started.

Behind the Scenes

How your clothes are cared for

Here's what happens between pickup and delivery — the stuff you don't see, but definitely feel.

📋

Sorting & Inspection

Every item is checked for stains, damage, and care labels. Clothes are sorted by color, fabric type, and wash method. Nothing goes in blind.

🫧

Pre-Treatment

Stains are individually treated with the right solvent. Collars and cuffs get extra attention. Delicates are set aside for hand-wash or dry clean.

🌊

Washing

Temperature-controlled cycles with premium detergents. Each load is optimized for the specific fabrics inside — no one-size-fits-all approach.

☀️

Drying

Tumble dry for cottons, air dry for delicates. Every item gets the method that keeps it looking and feeling its best. No shrinkage, guaranteed.

👔

Pressing & Finishing

Steam press or hand iron, based on the garment. Shirts are crisp, trousers are creased perfectly, and everything is lint-rolled for that finishing touch.

📦

Quality Check & Packaging

A final inspection ensures everything meets our standards. Items are neatly folded or hung, packed in eco-friendly bags, and labelled for your order.
